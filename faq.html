Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about tax filing, company registration, or legal compliance? Find the answers to the most common queries below.

Taxation & FBR Compliance

What documents are required for NTN registration?
For individuals, we typically need a copy of your CNIC, a registered mobile number, an email address, and proof of business premises (utility bill or tenancy agreement).
When is the deadline for annual income tax filing?
For most individuals and salaried persons, the deadline is September 30th each year. For companies, it depends on their specific tax year closing.

Business & SECP Registration

How long does it take to register a Private Limited Company?
With complete documentation, SECP registration usually takes 3 to 7 working days. This includes name reservation and issuance of the digital certificate.
Can a foreigner start a business in Pakistan?
Yes, foreigners can incorporate a company in Pakistan. However, it involves additional security clearance steps from the Ministry of Interior.
